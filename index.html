MFA curriculum map

Three specializations, term by term. Gold capsules hold courses that all three share. Select a course to see its prerequisites and what it unlocks. Solid outlines mark required links. Dashed outlines mark soft links: recommended, or not yet confirmed.

  • 42 units over six terms14 courses. Each course is 3 lecture hours per week.
  • Most courses have no prerequisiteThe curriculum lists "none" for all courses except the WCE and the thesis courses.
  • WCE gateTake the WCE after you complete at least 31 units.
  • Thesis chainThesis Writing I needs the WCE. Thesis Writing II and III need Thesis Writing I. III has II as a co-requisite.
  • Recommended: Research Methodology in the term before Thesis Writing IIt is recommended to take GS501 in the term right before Thesis Writing I. In the listed order, that is Year 2, Term 2. It is not a requirement, but it helps you start the thesis with momentum.
  • Journal publication (required)Publish at least one article in a refereed or indexed journal before graduation. Timing is not confirmed. It may fall during Thesis Writing I. One option is The Antoninus Journal of the UST Graduate School.
  • CognateAny course with substantial bearing on the thesis. You can choose a course outside the MFA, but it needs pre-approval.
  • Course codesFA600 to FA602 are core. FA61x is Painting and Sculpture, FA62x is Industrial Design, FA63x is Visual Communication.
